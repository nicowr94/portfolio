# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering teams evaluating Nicolas Wong for software engineering opportunities.

## Product Purpose

Present Nicolas Wong's software engineering experience, selected projects, and technical range so hiring teams can assess his fit and reach out about relevant opportunities. Success means a visitor can understand his experience and impact, then continue the conversation through LinkedIn.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents Nicolas as a Senior Full Stack Developer who works across product interfaces, backend services, data, and integrations, supported by examples of engineering work and its business impact.

## Operating Context

Visitors review a public, Spanish-language portfolio during a hiring search. They can scan selected projects, technical tools, and a career timeline with experience at Banco Pichincha, Pragma, and Outbuild. LinkedIn is the contact path.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site presents selected engineering projects, technical tools, career history, company experience, and a LinkedIn contact link.
- Nicolas is available in Lima for remote international work.
- Do not include a personal photo, downloadable CV, or email address.
- Keep project and career claims tied to the information already provided for the portfolio; do not invent customer results, endorsements, or metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the professional name Nicolas Wong and the title Senior Full Stack Developer.
- Spanish is the primary language of the portfolio.
- The current availability statement is “DISPONIBLE · LIMA / REMOTO INTERNACIONAL”.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current project content describes Outbuild work on baseline performance, Oracle Primavera P6 XML exports, and schedule change history.
- The portfolio currently reports baseline calculations improving from about 10 minutes to 6 seconds.
- The experience timeline lists Banco Pichincha, Pragma, and Outbuild with role descriptions and dates.
- No personal photo, CV, or email should be presented.

## Product Principles

- Help hiring teams assess relevant experience quickly.
- Ground claims in concrete project work and outcomes.
- Show full-stack scope across interfaces, services, data, and integrations.
- Respect the confirmed privacy boundaries.
